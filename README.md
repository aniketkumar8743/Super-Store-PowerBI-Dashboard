# Power BI Super Store Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides a **detailed analysis of Super Store sales performance**, including key sales metrics, trends, segment-wise breakdowns, and a **15-day sales forecast**. The dashboard helps in understanding sales distribution across categories, regions, and shipment methods while also offering a predictive analysis of future sales trends.

## 🚀 Key Features
### **1️⃣ Sales Performance Analysis**
- **Sales & Profit Overview:** Displays total sales, profit, and average shipping days.
- **Sales by Category & Sub-Category:** Breakdown of sales across different product categories.
- **Sales by Ship Mode:** Insights into the shipping methods and their contribution to total sales.
- **Sales by Segment & Payment Mode:** Understanding of customer segments and payment preferences.

### **2️⃣ Trend Analysis & Year-over-Year (YoY) Comparison**
- **Monthly Sales & Profit Analysis (YoY):** Comparison of sales and profit trends for 2019 & 2020.
- **State-wise Sales & Profit Distribution:** A map visualization showing geographical performance.
- **Segment-wise Profit Contribution:** A breakdown of profit contribution by customer segments.

### **3️⃣ Forecasting & Predictive Insights**
- **15-Day Sales Forecast:** Using historical data to predict future sales trends.
- **Time-Series Analysis:** Sales trends over the past two years to identify seasonal patterns.

## 📊 Insights & Findings
✅ **Standard Class is the Most Used Shipping Mode:** Contributes the highest sales.
✅ **Consumer Segment Drives Maximum Profit:** 48% of profit comes from the consumer segment.
✅ **Credit Card is the Most Popular Payment Mode:** 43% of transactions are COD, while 35% are online payments.
✅ **Sales Show a Seasonal Trend:** Peaks observed during year-end months (November-December).
✅ **California & New York Lead in Sales:** Followed by Texas and Washington.
✅ **Forecast Predicts a Slight Increase in Sales in the Next 15 Days.**

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
- **Power BI** (Data Visualization & Dashboarding)
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** (Calculations & Measures)
- **Power Query (M Language)** (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
- **Time Series Forecasting** (Sales Prediction)

## 📂 Dataset
The dataset includes transactional data with details such as:
- **Order Date, Ship Date, Sales, Profit, Category, Sub-Category, Region, Payment Mode, and Customer Segment.**

## 🔥 How to Use
1. Open the Power BI file (`.pbix`).
2. Load the dataset into Power BI.
3. Explore the dashboard to analyze sales insights.
4. Modify filters to focus on specific regions, categories, or time periods.
